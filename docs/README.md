> Set one big goal. Let your AI coach guide you there.

Milesto is an AI-powered personal coaching app for iOS. You pick the goal that matters most, choose a coach personality that suits you, and Milesto turns the rest into a personalized roadmap — milestones to aim for, weekly plans to keep you moving, and daily tasks small enough to actually finish.

## What's inside

- **iOS app** — the experience you live in every day.
- **API** — the brain that plans your roadmap and powers your coach.
- **Web** — public pages and an admin dashboard behind the scenes.

## Philosophy

One goal at a time. Real progress over busywork. A coach that adapts to you, not the other way around.
