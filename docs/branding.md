# Momentum Branding

## Brand essence

Momentum is an AI coaching app that helps people make meaningful progress on one important goal. It should feel like serious progress with emotional support.

## Positioning

- Category: AI personal coaching and goal execution app
- Audience: ambitious people who need help turning a big goal into consistent daily action
- Promise: turn ambition into a clear path with structure, support, and accountability
- Difference: not just tasks, not just chat; it combines planning, coaching, and follow-through

## Audience

Primary users:

- young professionals
- founders and creators
- students with serious goals
- career switchers
- self-improvement-oriented people who want structure, not just inspiration

## Emotional shift

- Before: overwhelmed, scattered, stuck, unsure what to do next
- After: clear, calm, directed, supported, capable

## Brand traits

- Should feel: focused, human, calm, energizing, credible
- Personality: grounded, motivating, intelligent
- Avoid: robotic, hypey, clinical

## Voice

- clear, not corporate
- supportive, not cheesy
- confident, not pushy
- smart, not academic
- calm, not passive

Brand voice should sound like a thoughtful coach: practical, encouraging, and emotionally intelligent.

## Visual direction

Momentum should feel like a modern coaching brand that combines calm clarity with forward motion.

- premium, warm, focused, structured, encouraging
- not hyper-techy
- not generic startup SaaS
- not cold productivity minimalism
- not loud self-improvement hype

## Color direction

Recommended first-pass palette:

- Primary: deep moss or forest green
- Secondary: warm sand or stone
- Neutrals: off-white, charcoal, muted gray
- Accent: muted amber or copper

This balances growth, steadiness, warmth, and energy.

## Typography direction

Recommended approach:

- Display: expressive serif for warmth, credibility, and character
- UI: clean sans-serif for clarity and usability

Suggested display fonts:

- Canela
- GT Super
- Tiempos Headline
- Newsreader
- Fraunces

Suggested UI fonts:

- Sohne
- Suisse Int'l
- Graphik
- Geist
- Plus Jakarta Sans

Suggested pairings:

- Canela + Sohne
- GT Super + Suisse Int'l
- Tiempos Headline + Graphik
- Newsreader + Geist

## Tagline directions

- Make real progress.
- One goal. Clear path.
- Turn ambition into momentum.
- Progress you can feel.

## Product feeling

When applied in the app, the brand should make the product feel:

- structured but not rigid
- supportive but not soft
- premium but not elitist
- motivating but not stressful
- modern but still human
