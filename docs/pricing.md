# Pricing Recommendation

Base country: France.

These prices are placeholders for positioning and paywall strategy. They are not based on live competitor pricing.

## Recommended Plans

| Plan    |            Price | Paywall display                  |
| ------- | ---------------: | -------------------------------- |
| Monthly | `14,99 € / mois` | Flexible access, cancel anytime  |
| Annual  |   `89,99 € / an` | `7,50 € / mois`, billed annually |

Default selected plan: **Annual**.

Annual badge: **Économise 50 %**.

Trial: **7-day free trial on annual only**.

## Strategy

Use two recurring plans only: monthly and annual.

The monthly plan should feel credible for a serious AI coaching product, not cheap or disposable. The annual plan should sit under the `100 €` psychological threshold while making the monthly equivalent feel like the obvious value choice.

This structure optimizes for perceived value, stronger upfront revenue, and lower churn while keeping the purchase decision simple.

## Do Not Offer

- No weekly subscription
- No lifetime subscription
- No quarterly plan unless testing later shows annual conversion is too low

## Suggested Paywall Framing

Lead with the annual plan as the best-value option:

- **Annual**: `89,99 € / an`
- Supporting line: `Seulement 7,50 € / mois`
- Badge: `Économise 50 %`
- Terms: `Gratuit pendant 7 jours, puis 89,99 € par an. Annulable à tout moment.`

Monthly remains the fallback for users who need flexibility:

- **Monthly**: `14,99 € / mois`
- Terms: `14,99 € par mois. Annulable à tout moment.`
